# Budgetly Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.2

**Effective date:** November 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Budgetly is a personal finance management application operated by Sophie Kendi, an independent developer based in Kenya. This Privacy Policy explains how Budgetly collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you visit the website, create an account, use financial tools, make a purchase, or contact Budgetly.

**Data controller:** Sophie Kendi
**Application:** Budgetly
**Country:** Kenya
**Email:** budgetly01@gmail.com

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account information

Budgetly may process your username, email address, first and last name where provided, password in securely hashed form, account settings and preferences, registration eligibility declaration, and records of acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy.

### 2.2 Google Sign-In information

You may use username/password authentication or Google Sign-In. If you choose Google Sign-In, Budgetly receives the verified email address, Google account subject identifier, and available given and family name information needed to create or link your Budgetly account. Budgetly does not store the Google ID token.

### 2.3 Financial information

Budgetly stores information you choose to record, including financial account names, currencies and starting balances; income, expenses and transaction details; categories and budgets; transfers; savings goals and activity; borrowing, lending, loan-interest, and repayment records; and financial notes and descriptions.

Some entries may contain information about other people, such as names recorded for personal loans. Avoid adding information about others unless reasonably necessary and lawfully obtained.

### 2.4 Support and communication

When you request support or submit feedback, Budgetly may process your contact information, message, feedback category, and relevant correspondence.

### 2.5 Payment information

Budgetly uses **Paystack** to process the KES 1,050 one-time lifetime-access purchase. The total customer payment charged by Budgetly at checkout is KES 1,050, and Budgetly does not add separate checkout charges. Budgetly retains limited payment-administration information, including the Budgetly purchase reference, expected amount and currency, payment status, provider transaction identifier, and non-sensitive provider-response details needed for reconciliation, fraud prevention, refund, or dispute handling.

Budgetly does not store full card, bank-account, or payment-PIN details. Paystack processes payment information under its own terms and privacy practices.

### 2.6 Browser and technical information

Budgetly uses browser storage to support account sessions and save preferences, including appearance and onboarding settings. Technical information may also be processed by infrastructure providers for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Budgetly uses personal information to register, authenticate, and manage accounts; provide financial tracking and reporting; calculate balances, budgets, savings progress, currency conversions, and loan figures; deliver welcome and password-recovery emails; administer payment access; respond to feedback and support requests; prevent unauthorized access; meet applicable legal obligations; and handle requests concerning personal information.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may be necessary to perform an agreement with you, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests that do not override your rights, or rely on consent where required. Acknowledging this Privacy Policy does not constitute blanket consent to unrelated processing.

## 4. Financial Information and Accuracy

Budgetly primarily processes records entered by users. It does not independently verify that recorded transactions, balances, loans, or repayments match those held by financial institutions or other individuals. The accuracy of financial reports depends on entered information and the application's calculation rules.

## 5. Service Providers

Budgetly uses third parties to deliver parts of the service:

- **Alwaysdata:** backend application hosting and related infrastructure.
- **Brevo:** transactional emails, including welcome and password-reset emails.
- **Google:** identity authentication when you choose Google Sign-In.
- **Paystack:** one-time lifetime-access payment processing.
- **Frankfurter:** exchange-rate data for supported currency conversions. Exchange-rate requests use currency information rather than an intentional transfer of your complete financial records.

Providers may process information needed to perform their services. Budgetly does not authorize them to use personal information for unrelated purposes merely because they support Budgetly.

## 6. International Processing

Budgetly is operated from Kenya and intended to be accessible internationally. Some providers may process or store information outside Kenya or the country where you live. Where international transfers occur, Budgetly will apply safeguards and legal requirements relevant to those transfers.

## 7. Cookies and Browser Storage

Budgetly uses session storage for authentication tokens and local storage for certain interface preferences. The frontend does not currently include third-party advertising pixels or analytics SDKs. Other technical cookies or identifiers may be used by supporting infrastructure.

If Budgetly introduces optional analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking, it will update disclosures and provide any consent mechanisms required by law.

## 8. Email Communications

Budgetly sends operational emails associated with your account, including welcome and password-reset messages. Budgetly does not currently use email addresses for promotional marketing campaigns. If it introduces marketing communications, it will provide appropriate information and choices, including consent where required.

## 9. Data Security

Budgetly uses measures intended to protect personal information, including password hashing, authenticated access to protected features, and controls limiting access to user-owned records. No online system is completely risk-free. Protect your password and notify Budgetly of suspected unauthorized access.

## 10. Data Retention

Budgetly retains personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

- **Account and financial records:** kept while needed to maintain an active account and provide requested services.
- **Deleted accounts:** successfully deleted accounts and associated user-owned financial records are removed from Budgetly's primary application database.
- **Payment records:** after account deletion, Budgetly may retain payment-reconciliation evidence with the deleted account link removed where reasonably necessary for lawful accounting, dispute, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.
- **Feedback and support records:** kept as needed to resolve requests and administer related issues.
- **Operational logs and backups:** retained only as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes and applicable legal obligations.

Deletion from the primary database may not immediately erase information held in backups, technical logs, delivered emails, or independent copies retained by service providers.

## 11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to be informed about use of your information; request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection where applicable; and withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. To make a request, email budgetly01@gmail.com. Budgetly may request reasonable information to verify identity before disclosing or changing personal records.

Where relevant, you may also contact Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at https://www.odpc.go.ke/.

## 12. Data Export and Account Deletion

Budgetly provides an account export covering categories supported by the current export tool. It may not include every type of personal information held by the service. You may request additional information through the privacy contact.

You can initiate account deletion through Settings. Password confirmation is required. Once successful, deletion removes the account and associated user-owned financial records from the primary database and is intended to be irreversible. Export records you wish to retain before deleting.

Residual copies and information subject to lawful retention are handled as described in this Policy. Payment-reconciliation evidence may remain without an associated Budgetly user account.

## 13. Age Requirements

Budgetly is intended for adults aged 18 or older. During registration, users must declare that they meet the minimum age requirement. Budgetly does not currently independently verify age or identity. If you believe someone under 18 has registered, contact budgetly01@gmail.com.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

Budgetly may update this Policy to reflect changes to services, data practices, or applicable law. Updated versions will show their effective date and version, and Budgetly will provide additional notice or seek consent when required.
